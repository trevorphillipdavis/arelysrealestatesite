## My Take

Looking across the latest data from Broward and Miami-Dade, the South Florida market isn't cooling down—it’s maturing. We are officially past the chaotic, over-leveraged bidding wars of previous years, but don't mistake that for a slowdown. Instead, a massive wave of cash and international wealth is establishing a permanent, higher baseline for our region. 

The big takeaway for buyers and sellers right now is the widening gap between single-family homes and condos. If you hold single-family inventory in Broward or Miami-Dade, you are sitting pretty with tight supply and steady appreciation. But if you're navigating the condo and townhome space, you have to contend with stricter milestone inspections, reserve fund mandates, and rising insurance costs. For buyers, mid-6% mortgage rates shouldn't scare you into sitting on the sidelines; with inventory up slightly and prices stabilizing, you actually have room to breathe, negotiate, and secure long-term value before the next wave hits.

### 1. South Florida Home Sales Surge as Luxury Buyers Pay Cash
Driven by intense wealth migration and heavy cash-buyer participation, home sales jumped significantly. Roughly 37% of Broward purchases and 38% of Miami-Dade purchases closed entirely in cash, far outpacing the national average.
* **Read more:** [Miami Herald Report](https://www.miamiherald.com/news/business/real-estate-news/)

### 2. Million-Dollar Plus Sales Jump 35% in Broward and 29% in Miami-Dade
Luxury tier single-family transactions saw a massive year-over-year expansion. High-end demand remains resilient and insulated against elevated mortgage rates due to the large volume of cash and affluent buyers entering the region.
* **Read more:** [World Red Eye Market Breakdown](https://worldredeye.com/2026/07/wre-news-luxury-buyers-fuel-south-florida-housing-rebound/)

### 3. Regional Report: High-End Sales Surge Across South Florida in Q2
Published by major regional brokerages, new Q2 data reveals that luxury single-family sales jumped 20.1% across the region to over 8,000 transactions, while high-end condo sales rose 23.5%.

### 4. Broward County Single-Family Inventory Holds Steady at a 4.5-Month Supply
Local market updates show single-family inventory in Broward sitting tightly under the six-month threshold, preserving a mild seller's edge while median prices remain stable near $630,000 to $645,000.

### 5. Miami-Dade Median Single-Family Home Price Reaches $695,000
Overall volume in Miami-Dade grew for the tenth consecutive month, with single-family prices climbing 3.7% year-over-year while days on market normalized, requiring sellers to keep pricing grounded and realistic.

### 6. The Divergence Between Single-Family Homes and Condos Across Broward & Miami-Dade
While single-family houses enjoy tight supply, the condo and townhome sectors face elevated inventories and cooling price tags—partly driven by stricter post-Surfside milestone building inspection and reserve funding mandates.
