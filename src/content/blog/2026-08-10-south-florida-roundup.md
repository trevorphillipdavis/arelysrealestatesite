## My Take

Looking across the latest data from Broward and Miami-Dade, the South Florida market continues to prove its maturity and long-term holding power. While buyers and sellers have successfully moved past the frantic, over-leveraged bidding wars of previous years, demand remains strong—particularly backed by robust cash-buyer participation and high-end wealth migration.

The divergence between single-family homes and condos remains the most critical trend to watch. Single-family inventory across Broward and Miami-Dade stays tightly constrained, protecting property values and providing steady appreciation. Meanwhile, condo and townhome sectors face more breathing room and rising inventory due to stricter milestone inspections and reserve fund mandates. For serious buyers, mid-6% mortgage rates shouldn't keep you on the sidelines; current stabilization offers an ideal window to negotiate and secure long-term value.

## Top Stories & Market Highlights

1. **South Florida Home Sales Surge as Luxury Buyers Pay Cash**
   - Driven by intense wealth migration and heavy cash-buyer participation, home sales jumped significantly. Roughly 37% of Broward purchases and 38% of Miami-Dade purchases closed entirely in cash, far outpacing the national average.
   - [Read the Miami Herald Report](https://www.miamiherald.com/news/business/real-estate-news/)

2. **Million-Dollar Plus Sales Jump 35% in Broward and 29% in Miami-Dade**
   - Luxury tier single-family transactions saw a massive year-over-year expansion. High-end demand remains resilient and insulated against elevated mortgage rates due to the large volume of cash and affluent buyers entering the region.
   - [Read the World Red Eye Market Breakdown](https://worldredeye.com/)

3. **Regional Report: High-End Sales Surge Across South Florida in Q2**
   - Published by major regional brokerages, new Q2 data reveals that luxury single-family sales jumped 20.1% across the region to over 8,000 transactions, while high-end condo sales rose 23.5%.
   - [Read the RISMedia Regional Report](https://www.rismedia.com/)

4. **Broward County Single-Family Inventory Holds Steady at a 4.5-Month Supply**
   - Local market updates show single-family inventory in Broward sitting tightly under the six-month threshold, preserving a mild seller's edge while median prices remain stable near $630,000 to $645,000.
   - [Read the DiscoverSouthFL Broward Market Update](https://discoversouthfl.com/)

5. **Miami-Dade Median Single-Family Home Price Reaches $695,000**
   - Overall volume in Miami-Dade grew for the tenth consecutive month, with single-family prices climbing 3.7% year-over-year while days on market normalized, requiring sellers to keep pricing grounded and realistic.
   - [Read the Divito Real Estate Group Analysis](https://divitorealestategroup.com/)

6. **The Divergence Between Single-Family Homes and Condos Across Broward & Miami-Dade**
   - While single-family houses enjoy tight supply, the condo and townhome sectors face elevated inventories and cooling price tags—partly driven by stricter post-Surfside milestone building inspection and reserve funding mandates.
   - [Read the Real Listing Agent South Florida Report](https://reallistingagent.com/)

7. **Salary Requirements to Buy or Rent Hit Record Highs in Miami-Dade**
   - Recent economic affordability metrics published this week indicate that buyers and renters need a baseline salary approaching six figures to comfortably secure standard multi-bedroom housing inventory across urban core neighborhoods.
   - [Read the Miami Herald Business & Real Estate Section](https://www.miamiherald.com/)

8. **Billionaire Real Estate Expansion Continues in Downtown Miami and Miami Beach**
   - Major land assemblies and high-profile property buyouts by tech and finance titans—including massive block acquisitions near Biscayne Bay—continue to command local headlines, reshaping the downtown luxury commercial and residential landscape.
   - [Read the Miami Herald Development News](https://www.miamiherald.com/)

9. **Navigating Short-Term Rental Regulations and HOA Disputes in South Florida Condos**
   - Regional property associations are cracking down harder on unauthorized short-term leases, prompting legal updates and new guidance for investors managing multi-family units in core tourism submarkets like Brickell.
   - [Read the Miami Herald HOA & Legal Coverage](https://www.miamiherald.com/)

10. **Mortgage Rate Stabilization Keeps Buyers Active Despite Narrow Rate Fluctuations**
    - With 30-year fixed mortgage rates hovering in the mid-6% range, local real estate analysts are advising buyers against waiting on the sidelines for steep rate drops, emphasizing that steady home prices offer ideal entry points for long-term buyers.
    - [Read the DiscoverSouthFL Market Insights](https://discoversouthfl.com/)
