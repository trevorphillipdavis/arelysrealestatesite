## 🏘️ Real Estate Market Snapshot

- **Median Sale Price & Growth:** The median home price in Davie holds steady around $575,000, reflecting continued steady year-over-year appreciation as family-oriented housing demand remains resilient across Broward County.
- **Days on Market:** Properties are averaging approximately 65 to 70 days on the market, with well-priced single-family homes moving swiftly in the $500K to $850K range.
- **Inventory & Activity:** While new construction options support higher-end segments, standard resale inventory in established Davie communities remains low, keeping competition active for prospective buyers.
