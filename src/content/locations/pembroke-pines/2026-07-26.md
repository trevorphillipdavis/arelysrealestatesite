## 🏘️ Real Estate Market Snapshot (Late July 2026)

* **Median Sale Price:** Single-family home prices in Pembroke Pines hold stable around **$495,000 to $505,000**, reflecting steady, resilient buyer demand across Broward County.
* **Days on Market:** Properties are averaging roughly **37 to 71 days on market**, giving active buyers a bit more negotiating space than in prior hyper-competitive years.
* **Inventory & Options:** While single-family houses maintain strong valuations, townhome and condo inventories are seeing gradual climbs, offering alternative entry points for local buyers.
