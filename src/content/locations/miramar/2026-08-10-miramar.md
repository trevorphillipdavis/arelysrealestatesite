## 🏘️ Real Estate Market Snapshot

- **Market Balance & Pricing:** Miramar continues to operate as a well-balanced housing market with steady supply and demand dynamics, averaging sales at roughly 2.18% below asking price and a stable sale-to-list price ratio near 98%.
- **Days on Market:** Properties across the city are moving at a moderate pace with a median timeframe of approximately 64 days, offering active buyers adequate time for evaluation and negotiation.
- **Inventory & Activity:** While median price points span diverse micro-markets—from accessible condo options in Lakeshore to single-family homes in upscale enclaves like Saphire Shores—broader sales activity across Broward County demonstrates healthy, sustained buyer participation.
